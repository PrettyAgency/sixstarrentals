Overview

A flagship foldable and studio-grade audio, on one weekly payment

The Samsung Galaxy Z Flip5 folds a 6.7-inch flagship phone down to something that disappears into a pocket. Open it for full-size video, gaming and multitasking; keep it closed and the 3.4-inch Flex Window handles messages, music, maps and selfies without unfolding at all.

Because the Flex Hinge holds the phone at any angle, it becomes its own tripod — hands-free video calls, night shots without shake, and time-lapse from a bench or table. Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 keeps everything responsive, and the IPX8 rating means a splash or a shower of rain is not a crisis.

Paired with it are HAVIT Space S1 over-ear headphones with active noise cancelling and up to 40 hours of playback — enough to get through a working week of commutes, study sessions or shift work on a single charge. Bluetooth 5.3 pairs instantly with the Flip5, and the built-in microphone handles calls and voice assistants.

Rent the pair from Six Star Rentals and there is no deposit, no lump sum and no surprise fees. Choose weekly or fortnightly payments over 12 or 24 months, and when your final payment lands, that is the end of it.

FAQs

Questions we're asked every day

What does “Near New” mean for the Galaxy Z Flip5?
It means a Grade A refurbished handset rather than a brand-new one. There are no cracks, dents or screen marks visible at arm's length in normal light, the battery health has been verified, and the phone has passed our full 5-point function test. The HAVIT Space S1 headphones in this bundle are brand new in a sealed box. Being upfront about condition is how we keep the weekly payment low.
Is a deposit or bond required?
No. There is no deposit and no bond on any Six Star Rentals agreement. Your first payment is simply your first scheduled rental payment.
Can I apply if I receive Centrelink payments?
Yes. Part of our promise is giving everyone a fair go. We accept applications from customers receiving parenting payment, family tax benefit, carer allowance, disability support pension, widow allowance, age pension and JobSeeker, and we assess your circumstances fairly and responsibly.
What if my credit or rental history has been poor?
We consider every application individually. People go through hard times, and we look at where you are today rather than ruling you out on history alone. It costs nothing to ask.
Will a credit check be done?
Yes — we run an Equifax credit check as part of assessing your application, and only after you have given us your consent. We will never run one without asking you first.
What documents do I need to apply?
Photo ID (driver licence or passport) and your income details — recent payslips or a Centrelink income statement. Have your delivery address and a contact number handy too.
How do I apply?
Add the bundle to your cart and follow the prompts, or call us on 1800 678 277 and one of our team will do it with you over the phone. You can also request a call back and we'll ring you, Monday to Friday between 8:00am and 5:00pm.
How much is delivery and how long will it take?
Delivery is free anywhere in Australia, tracked and insured in transit. Once your application is approved we'll confirm your dispatch date and send tracking details by SMS and email so you know exactly when to expect it.
Will the phone work with my current carrier and number?
Yes. The Galaxy Z Flip5 is unlocked and 5G-ready, so it works with every Australian carrier — Telstra, Optus, Vodafone and all the resellers on their networks. Move your SIM across and keep your existing number and plan.
Who pays for repairs if something goes wrong?
If it's normal wear and tear or a breakdown, it's covered by the warranty for the life of your agreement and you pay nothing. Call us and we'll arrange the repair or replacement. Accidental damage and loss aren't covered by warranty, so ask us about your options if that happens.
Can I end my rental early, or what if my circumstances change?
Yes, you can end the agreement early — there may be fees and charges, so call us and we'll give you an exact payout figure before you decide. If you're experiencing financial hardship, please call us early. We understand circumstances change and we'll listen and work through it with you.
What happens when I make my last payment?
We contact you before your final payment so there are no surprises. You can return the goods, or take up our gift to family or friends offer — we gift the bundle to a person you nominate (anyone other than you: a partner, family member, friend or someone in your household) with nothing more to pay. You can nominate that person at the start and change them at any time.
